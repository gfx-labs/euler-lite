What is Poppie?

Liquidity for
your stocks

Poppie is a decentralized lending protocol purpose-built for tokenized equities. It connects your stock portfolio to the world of DeFi, letting you borrow USDT against blue-chip stocks, ETFs, and more, all on BNB Smart Chain.

Why We Built Poppie

Your stocks shouldn't be dead capital

Millions of people hold tokenized securities but can't do anything with them besides watch the price. Traditional brokerages offer margin loans, but they're slow, opaque, and gated behind paperwork.

We built Poppie because DeFi lending already solved this for crypto assets. Aave, Compound, and others let you borrow against ETH and stablecoins instantly. But there was nothing purpose-built for the growing world of tokenized real-world assets.

Poppie bridges that gap. Same battle-tested Euler V2 infrastructure. Same instant, permissionless experience. But designed from the ground up for equities, with risk tiers that make sense for stocks, oracle feeds tuned to market hours, and a curated set of 37 assets you actually want to hold.
