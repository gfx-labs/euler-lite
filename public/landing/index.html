What is Poppie?

DeFi lending for
real-world assets

Poppie is a decentralized lending protocol purpose-built for tokenized equities. It connects your stock portfolio to the world of DeFi, letting you borrow USDT against blue-chip stocks, ETFs, and more — all on BNB Smart Chain.

For Stock Holders

You own tokenized equities through Ondo Finance and want to put them to work without selling. Deposit as collateral, borrow what you need.

For DeFi Users

You know DeFi but want exposure to traditional markets. Poppie brings Wall Street assets on-chain with the composability you expect.

For Institutions

Need capital-efficient access to tokenized securities? Poppie offers transparent, auditable lending with on-chain risk parameters.

How It Works

Three steps to liquidity

1

Deposit your tokenized stocks

Connect your wallet and deposit Ondo-tokenized equities like AAPL, NVDA, SPY, or any of 21 supported assets as collateral.

2

Borrow USDT instantly

Borrow stablecoins against your collateral at competitive rates. No credit checks, no paperwork, no waiting periods.

3

Keep your exposure

Your stocks stay deposited and maintain full price exposure. Repay your loan anytime to withdraw. You never miss a rally.

Why We Built Poppie

Your stocks shouldn't be dead capital

Millions of people hold tokenized securities but can't do anything with them besides watch the price. Traditional brokerages offer margin loans, but they're slow, opaque, and gated behind paperwork.

We built Poppie because DeFi lending already solved this for crypto assets — Aave, Compound, and others let you borrow against ETH and stablecoins instantly. But there was nothing purpose-built for the growing world of tokenized real-world assets.

Poppie bridges that gap. Same battle-tested Euler V2 infrastructure. Same instant, permissionless experience. But designed from the ground up for equities — with risk tiers that make sense for stocks, oracle feeds tuned to market hours, and a curated set of 21 assets you actually want to hold.
