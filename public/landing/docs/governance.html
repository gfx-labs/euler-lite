Governance

Poppie's protocol configuration on Euler V2 is managed by the Poppie team. All parameters are set onchain, publicly verifiable, and subject to a timelock.

Who Manages Poppie?

The Poppie team at Oku Trade manages the protocol's configuration on Euler V2. This includes setting collateral parameters (LTV ratios), vault caps, interest rate models, and oracle configuration.

Every configuration change is executed through onchain transactions on BNB Smart Chain. This means all current parameters and any changes to them are publicly visible and independently verifiable by anyone.

Timelock

All parameter changes to the Poppie protocol go through a timelock contract with a minimum delay of 1 day. This means that when the Poppie team proposes a change (for example, adjusting an LTV ratio or modifying a vault cap), the change cannot take effect for at least 24 hours after it is submitted onchain.

During this delay period, anyone can see the pending change on the blockchain. This gives users time to review proposed changes and adjust their positions if needed before they go into effect.

Coming soon: We plan to offer an email notification service so users can receive alerts whenever a parameter change is proposed. This will make it even easier to stay informed without needing to monitor the blockchain directly.

Collateral LTV Ratios

Each collateral asset has two LTV (loan-to-value) thresholds:

  • Borrow LTV is the maximum percentage of your collateral's value that you can borrow. For example, a 65% Borrow LTV means you can borrow up to $6,500 against $10,000 of collateral.
  • Liquidation LTV is the threshold at which your position becomes eligible for liquidation. If your loan value exceeds this percentage of your collateral's value, liquidators can step in.

LTV ratios are set conservatively based on asset characteristics. Broad ETFs (SPY, QQQ) receive the highest LTVs because they are less volatile. Mega-cap stocks (AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL) receive moderate LTVs. More volatile or concentrated assets (MSTR, RKLB) receive the most conservative LTVs.

Verifying Parameters Yourself

Because Poppie is built on Euler V2 and deployed on a public blockchain, you do not need to take our word for any of the values listed above. You can verify every parameter independently:

  • View the USDT vault configuration directly on BscScan
  • Check the timelock contract and any pending proposals at its address on BscScan
  • Review LTV configurations through the Euler protocol's public contract reads

Transparency is a core principle of Poppie. If a parameter listed on this page ever falls out of date, the onchain values are always the source of truth.
