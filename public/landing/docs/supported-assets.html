Understanding Market Hours

Poppie's availability is tied to the liquidity of each asset's underlying market. Since these are tokenized versions of US equities, pricing follows US stock market hours.

Trading sessions

US equity markets operate across four sessions on weekdays:

SessionHours (Eastern Time)Days
Pre-market4:00 AM – 9:30 AMMon–Fri
Regular9:30 AM – 4:00 PMMon–Fri
Post-market4:00 PM – 8:00 PMMon–Fri
Overnight8:00 PM – 4:00 AMSun–Thu

What "24/5" means

Most assets on Poppie (35 out of 37) have 24/5 availability. This means prices are available from Sunday 8:00 PM ET through Friday 8:00 PM ET, covering all four trading sessions back to back across the work week.

During these hours, you can borrow additional funds, withdraw collateral from open positions, and liquidations can occur.

Regular-hours-only assets

NFLXon and RKLBon only have pricing during regular market hours (9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET, Monday through Friday). Outside these hours, these assets cannot be used for new borrows or collateral withdrawals from open positions.

Weekends

From Friday 8:00 PM ET to Sunday 8:00 PM ET, no US equity trading sessions are active. During this time:

  • No new prices are pushed (this is expected, not an error)
  • New borrows and collateral withdrawals from open positions are paused
  • You can still deposit additional collateral or repay loans
  • You can withdraw collateral if you have no open loan
  • Everything resumes automatically when markets reopen

Federal holidays: When US stock markets are closed for holidays (e.g., Independence Day, Thanksgiving), the same rules as weekends apply. No new prices are available, so borrow and withdrawal operations pause until markets reopen.

Practical Examples

Tuesday at 11 PM ET: you want to borrow more against NVDAon

NVDAon is a Tier A asset with 24/5 coverage. At 11 PM ET on a Tuesday, the overnight session is active, so fresh prices are available. You can borrow additional USDT against your NVDAon collateral.

Saturday afternoon: you want to add collateral

It's the weekend, so no prices are being updated. However, you can still deposit additional collateral. Adding collateral doesn't require a price check because it only improves your position. You just can't borrow more or withdraw collateral from an open loan until markets reopen.

Wednesday at 7 AM ET: you want to borrow against RKLBon

RKLBon is a regular-hours-only asset. At 7 AM, the pre-market session is active for most stocks, but RKLB only has regular-session pricing. You'll need to wait until 9:30 AM ET when regular hours begin.
