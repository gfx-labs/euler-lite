What is Poppie?

Poppie is a decentralized lending protocol built specifically for tokenized equities. It lets you deposit Ondo Global Markets tokenized stocks (AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, SPY, and more) as collateral and borrow USDT against them on BNB Smart Chain (BSC).

No paperwork. No credit checks. No origination fees. Connect your wallet, deposit your tokens, and borrow in seconds.

New to Poppie? Start with Why Poppie to understand the motivation, then head to How to Use Poppie for a step-by-step walkthrough.

Key Facts

  • 37 tokenized assets supported as collateral (Ondo Global Markets tokens on BSC)
  • Borrow USDT against your stock portfolio without selling
  • No origination fees. You only pay the borrowing interest rate
  • 24/5 market access for most assets (pre-market through overnight, Monday–Friday)
  • Built on Euler V2, battle-tested, audited lending infrastructure
  • Chainlink Data Streams for real-time price feeds during market hours
  • Permissionless. No KYC, no accounts, no approvals required
