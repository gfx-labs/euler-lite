Poppie Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how GFX Labs, Inc. d/b/a Oku Trade (“GFX,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you access or use Poppie, the non-custodial interface to lending and borrowing markets built on the Euler protocol on the BNB Smart Chain that we make available at poppie.io and related locations (“Poppie”).

Poppie is a product of GFX Labs, Inc., the same entity that operates Oku Trade. This Privacy Policy is incorporated into and made part of the Poppie Terms and Conditions and follows the same approach as the Oku Trade Privacy Policy; where this Policy is silent, the Oku Trade Privacy Policy describes our general practices. If there is a direct conflict regarding information processing in connection with Poppie, this Policy controls.

Because Poppie is designed for decentralized-finance use, much of what you do through it is recorded on public blockchains or handled by third parties (such as wallet providers, blockchain networks, and the Euler protocol). This Policy covers information we process (or that service providers process on our behalf) in connection with Poppie, not information processed solely by blockchains or third parties acting independently.

Important notices. We do not custody or control your crypto assets, and we do not collect your private keys or seed phrase.

Transactions and wallet activity in the Poppie Markets are recorded on public blockchains and may be visible to anyone, regardless of what we do. The Risk Parameters and Oracle configuration are also recorded on-chain and are publicly viewable.

We do not monitor your positions, collateralization, or liquidation thresholds for your benefit, and we do not notify you of parameter or oracle changes; any indicators or alerts may be delayed or unavailable.

1. What This Policy Covers

This Policy covers information processed by or on behalf of GFX in connection with Poppie. It does not cover information collected by third parties you interact with through Poppie (such as wallet providers, blockchain networks, the Euler protocol, or the issuers of Tokenized Assets), whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

We collect information you choose to provide, such as your email address (if you subscribe to updates or contact support), the content of support requests and correspondence, and records of your acceptance of the Poppie Terms (such as the wallet address used to accept, and the date/time and version accepted).

2.2 Wallet and onchain activity

When you connect a wallet or initiate an onchain interaction through Poppie, we may collect or generate your public wallet address and related identifiers; transaction hashes and limited transaction metadata (such as chain, timestamp, and status) for transactions you initiate through Poppie; and public onchain data we retrieve and display (such as balances, positions, supplied or borrowed amounts, collateralization, and Poppie Market data), which may be associated with your wallet address.

2.3 Device, network, and usage data

We and our service providers may automatically collect device and browser information; network information (such as IP address) and approximate location derived from IP address; and log, diagnostic, and usage data (such as access times, pages viewed, actions taken, errors, performance data, and API request metadata), including cookie and similar-technology data.

2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not intentionally collect your wallet private keys, seed phrase, or similar credentials; payment card numbers; or government-issued identification, biometric data, or similar sensitive identifiers, unless a third-party service you use requires it under its own policies.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to: provide and operate Poppie and the Poppie Markets, including enabling wallet connections and displaying onchain data; configure and maintain the Risk Parameters and operate, secure, and maintain the Oracle; improve and develop Poppie (including troubleshooting, testing, and analytics); protect Poppie and its users, prevent abuse, and detect fraud; comply with legal obligations, including sanctions and geographic-restriction compliance and, where applicable, determining eligibility for and applying restrictions on access to Poppie or particular Poppie Markets or Tokenized Assets (which may involve information such as IP address, derived location, and wallet address); and communicate with you, including responding to support requests and, if you opt in, sending updates.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information: with service providers that help us operate, secure, host, analyze, and support Poppie (including the operation of the Oracle, such as hosting and infrastructure for off-chain data sources and keeper processes); with blockchain networks and the Euler protocol as a consequence of your actions and of operating the Poppie Markets (for example, when transactions or parameter or oracle updates are submitted on-chain, where they may be processed by third-party nodes, relays, or indexers and become publicly visible); with affiliates and corporate-group entities for internal operations and compliance; for legal and safety reasons; in connection with a business transaction; and with your consent or at your direction. We do not sell your personal information for money.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We and our service providers may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to operate Poppie, remember preferences, measure performance, and understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled. Because there is no uniform standard for “Do Not Track” signals, we do not respond to them as a standalone setting, but we generally treat Global Privacy Control signals as a preference where feasible, and we honor applicable opt-out rights where required by law.

6. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can generally use Poppie without creating an account. If a feature requires a wallet connection, you can disconnect your wallet through your wallet provider and stop using Poppie at any time; disconnecting does not remove public onchain records. If you opt in to non-essential communications, you can opt out at any time. Depending on where you live and to the extent applicable law applies, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port certain personal information, and to opt out of certain processing. To exercise applicable rights, email us at legal@gfxlabs.io with enough information for us to verify your request. We provide any rights described here only to the extent required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention; Security; International Transfers; Children; Changes; Contact

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Poppie, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements; public blockchain data (including onchain parameter and oracle records) is retained onchain according to the relevant network’s operation and is generally not controlled by us. We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We are based in the United States, and information may be processed in the United States and other jurisdictions where we or our service providers operate. Poppie is not directed to, and is not intended for use by, anyone who is not the age of majority in their jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect information from such individuals. We may update this Policy from time to time and will revise the “Last updated” date above. Questions or requests: legal@gfxlabs.io.
